Declaration

Before you submit your application, you must agree to the following statements.

I confirm that:

  • I have the authority to complete this application on behalf of my organisation
  • the information I have provided in this application is complete, accurate and true to the best of my knowledge
  • if approved, I agree that the organisation will comply with the duties of a lead sponsor organisation
  • has a governance board where the majority of members are Vritish or Irish citizens
  • has a complaints procedure
  • has data protection and safeguarding policies

I agree to:

  • tell the Home Office if there is a change to the organisation, key personnel or any information relevant to this application before a decision is made
  • comply with requests for information from the Home Office, including checks on compliance, monitoring and evaluation of the sponsorship arrangement

I understand that:

  • if I knowingly make any false representations or submit false documents, the application will be refused and I may be prosecuted
  • it is an offence under section 25 of the Immigration Act 1971 to commit an act which facilitates a breach or attempted breach of immigration law
  • the Home Office may carry out checks to verify the information provided in the application and may seek information from relevant third parties, where necessary to assess the application
  • the information provided will be treated in confidence and will only be used for a lawful purpose permitted under UK data protection laws
  • the details of approved lead sponsor organisations will be published on GOV.UK
  • the Home Office may carry out on-site visits as part of its ongoing monitoring and assurance process

You will not receive a copy of this form after you submit it. Print this page or save a copy for your records.
